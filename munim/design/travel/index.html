Policy · set by the travel team (example)

Proof of failureBothTwo records must agree. A driver's, airline's or passenger's word alone is not enough.
Bus failed on the wayBusFull fare back within 24 h. No boarding scan: the no-show rule applies.
Who pays for a busBusPaytm refunds first, then recovers from the operator's payout.
Airline has paid PaytmFlightPass it on within 24 h. Never hold a passenger's money.
Airline has not paid in 7 daysFlightClaim with the airline, with proof. Tell the passenger the real status. Paying first is a business call.
Not in this mockupBothLate arrival, replacement bus, rescheduled flights, partial trips.
